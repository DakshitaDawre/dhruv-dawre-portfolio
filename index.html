About Me

Building a language
in stitch & pixel

"Design isn't just what it looks like - it's the story you tell before the person even touches the fabric."

Hey, I'm Dhruv - a 21-year-old knitwear designer and graphic artist currently in my 4th semester at NIFT, Bhopal. My work lives at the crossing point of tactile craft and digital aesthetics.

I'm obsessed with the architecture of knitwear - how a stitch structure can make a garment feel heavy or weightless, structured or fluid. That same thinking drives my graphic work: every layout, typeface choice, and colour decision is load-bearing.

Currently exploring oversized silhouettes, tonal colour-block knits, and editorial graphic systems that blur the line between fashion and visual art.

Creative Process

Tools & Workflow

01
🧶
Garment Construction
Merino, mohair, cotton blends - sourced and swatched before any needle touches the machine. Weight, drape, and handle inform every design decision.
02
✏️
Material Handling
Copic markers, fine liners, layout paper. Every collection concept lives on paper first - loose croquis, detail studies, drape experiments.
03
💻
Illustration
Adobe Illustrator for flat sketches & tech packs, Photoshop for mood collages, and Procreate on iPad for expressive editorial illustration.
04
📌
Moodboarding
Physical boards with fabric swatches, torn references, washi tape, and colour chips - always the anchor before a collection moves to samples.
05
🔢
Design Process
Hand-drafted stitch charts on graph paper, then digitalised. Cable structures, intarsia motifs, and texture patterns all begin on the grid.
